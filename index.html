🍎 The Little Apple and the Girl with Dimples

Once upon a time, ek chhota sa Apple tha, jo ek khoobsurat garden mein rehta tha. Wahan bahut saare flowers the, butterflies thi, aur aasmaan mein chhote-chhote sitare.

Lekin us garden ki sabse special cheez thi ek pyaari si Dimple. Jab woh hansti, toh lagta jaise saare flowers ek saath khil uthe hon. Apple ko uske cheeks ke woh do chhote dimples duniya ke sabse precious treasures lagte the.

Ek din Apple se anjaane mein ek galti ho gayi, aur Dimple udaas ho gayi. Garden mein sab kuch waisa hi tha, par Apple ko sab khaali-khaali lag raha tha.

Usne ek flower se poochha, “Tum itne khoobsurat ho, phir bhi aaj udaas kyun ho?”

Flower ne kaha, “Kyunki tumhari favourite smile aaj khil nahi rahi.”

Apple ko samajh aa gaya. Woh ek little flower leke Dimple ke paas gaya. Pehle usne kaan pakadne ki koshish ki, par phir yaad aaya ki Apple ke toh kaan hote hi nahi! Toh usne apni chhoti si dandi (stem) hi pakad li, aur bola,

“Sorry na... maafi de do. Ye dekho, maafi ke saath ek flower bhi laaya hoon, aur ek tight wali hug bhi baaki hai. Main hamesha tumhari smile ki wajah banne ki koshish karunga. Main tumhara khayal rakhunga, har din, har haal mein. Main karunga, sab kuch karunga.”

Dimple ne bina kuch kahe Apple ki taraf dekha.

Aur Apple ne dheere se kaha, “Mujhe tumhare dimples se zyada pyaari woh smile hai jo unhe banati hai.”

Phir thoda sharmaate hue, dheemi si awaaz mein bola, “Aur sach bolun? Jab tum hansti ho na, toh Apple ka dil dhak-dhak karne lagta hai. Isliye toh Apple itna laal hai... sab tumhari wajah se!”

Ab zara ruko... aur ek baar sochke dekho, abhi tumhare chehre par kya hai?

Shayad ek chhoti si smile?

Aur ye kya... gaal bhi halke se laal ho gaye? Dekho, ab Apple ke saath-saath Dimple bhi blush kar rahi hai.

Aur yahi dekhke, Apple bhi muskura raha hai.

Kyuki Apple ki duniya ka sabse khoobsurat pal wahi hai, jab Dimple muskuraati hai aur sharma jaati hai.

The End?

Nahi... kyunki Apple ko tumhare saath abhi bahut saari beautiful memories banani hain. 🍎❤️
